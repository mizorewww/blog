Welcome to the first multilingual demo post on `mizorewww`.

The blog now supports writing posts in separate language folders:

- Chinese posts live in `data/blog/zh/`
- English posts live in `data/blog/en/`
- Translations of the same article use the same filename, such as `multilingual-demo.mdx`

This English article is available at `/en/blog/multilingual-demo`. Its Chinese version is available at `/zh/blog/multilingual-demo`. The language switcher in the Header keeps the current path, so readers can jump between both versions directly.

When you write a new post, copy this file pair and update the title, summary, tags, and body.
